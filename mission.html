Our Mission

We have a big dream for our auto maintenance business. We don't just want to be good enough; we want to become the shining example of progress and excellence in the automotive world, starting right here in Botswana and eventually reaching across continents.

We are set on growing into a strong international organization that makes a real difference far beyond our borders. We believe in steady, smart expansion that brings pride to our country while building something lasting on the global stage.

We are determined to work hand in hand with famous vehicle manufacturers, helping to create the next generation of cars. This means embracing the newest technologies and always choosing solutions that are kinder to our environment.

One of our key ambitions is to start making our own replacement parts and toolkits. By doing this ourselves, we can cut down on costly imports, reach the highest international quality standards, and one day proudly export our products to other countries.

We plan to train many more technicians who are not only masters of modern repair tools but who also deeply care about eco-friendly ways of working, ensuring every job we do helps protect the planet.

We will move forward in four thoughtful stages. First, we focus on building a rock-solid base by improving our workshops, adopting advanced equipment, and investing heavily in training our team while exploring local manufacturing opportunities.

Our mission is to set up our own parts production, and grow our reputation as a reliable, forward-thinking company that delivers outstanding service and quality products.

We will form partnerships with major global brands, take part in vehicle development projects, and begin sending our automotive parts to customers in other nations.
