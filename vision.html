Our Vision

We aim to not just be up to the standards of the car maintenance but to be the epitome of evolution around the globe; to expand beyond Botswana to be an intercontinental organization.

We aim to take part in building vehicles alongside various renowned motorvehicle brands; to be involved in up to date technological standards and environmental friendly machines.

We aim to manufacture our own replacement parts and toolkits to reduce importaion and reach the international standard to export to other nations.

We aim to train more auto repair technicians that adhere to eco-friendly practices and proficient and competent to operate cutting edge auto-repair technology.
